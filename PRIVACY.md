# Privacy Policy for Retro Player

**Effective Date:** May 3, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Retro Player** ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our Chrome Extension. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent user experience.

## 2. Data Collection

**Personal Data:** Retro Player **does not collect**, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or location.

**Usage Data:** Our extension operates locally on your browser. We do not track your browsing history or collect data on how you interact with specific websites.

**Permissions:** The extension may request permissions (such as tabs or activeTab) solely to provide core functionality, such as controlling media playback. This data is processed locally and never sent to our servers.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. Since we do not collect user data, there is no data to share.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Retro Player does not integrate with third-party analytics or advertising platforms that track user behavior.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository: [https://github.com/Kyojtan/retro_player](https://github.com/Kyojtan/retro_player).
